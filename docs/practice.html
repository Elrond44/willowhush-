Meditation timer

Sit for as long as you like.

Choose a length, press begin, and let a soft singing-bowl bell mark the start and the end. Add interval bells if you like a gentle check-in along the way.

  • Bells are made in your browser with Web Audio, so turn your volume up a little.
  • Your chosen length and interval are remembered on this device.
  • The breathing glow in the centre is optional company, not a pace to follow.

Breathing circle

Let the circle lead the breath.

Four rhythms to choose from. Box breathing steadies, 4·7·8 and the long exhale soften. Breathe through your nose if you can, and stop if you feel light-headed.

Guided body scan

Travel slowly from crown to toes.

Eleven gentle stops, each highlighted on the figure as you go. Let it run at your own pace or step through by hand; switch on read-aloud to close your eyes.

  • Use the arrows (or your keyboard's arrow keys) to move between areas.
  • Choose how long to rest in each place.
  • A quiet chime marks each new area.

5-4-3-2-1 grounding

Come back through your senses.

When your thoughts are racing, name five things you see, four you feel, three you hear, two you smell, and one you taste. Tap once for each thing you notice.

  • Typing is optional, and nothing you type is saved or sent.
  • There's no wrong answer; small things count.
  • Skip any sense that isn't available to you.

Loving-kindness flow

Warm wishes, in widening circles.

Traditional metta phrases appear slowly, one at a time, as you offer them to yourself, a loved one, a neutral person, someone difficult, and finally all beings.

  • Choose the full journey, or one circle.
  • Write your own phrases; they're saved only on this device.
  • Read-aloud uses your browser's built-in voice.
