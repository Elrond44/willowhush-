Breathwork · 3 minutes

Three-minute breath

A short reset for any moment of the day. Follow the breath, soften the body, and come back to the still water.

Read the guidance
  1. 0:00Welcome. Settle into a comfortable seat, and let your hands rest.
  2. 0:10If it feels right, let your eyes close, or soften your gaze.
  3. 0:19Take a slow breath in through your nose.
  4. 0:26And let it go, a little longer on the way out.
  5. 0:34Again. Breathing in, the belly gently rising.
  6. 0:42Breathing out, the shoulders softening.
  7. 0:51Now let the breath find its own rhythm. There's no need to control it.
  8. 1:04Notice where you feel it most. The nose, the chest, or the belly.
  9. 1:18Rest your attention there, like a hand resting on still water.
  10. 1:34When the mind wanders, and it will, simply notice, and return.
  11. 1:50Each return is the practice. Nothing has gone wrong.
  12. 2:04Breathing in, I know I am breathing in.
  13. 2:14Breathing out, I know I am breathing out.
  14. 2:26Let one more breath be slow and full.
  15. 2:35And a long, unhurried breath out.
  16. 2:44Gently open your eyes. Carry this steadiness with you.
  17. 2:54Thank you for practicing.

Body scan · 5 minutes

Five-minute body scan

A slow walk of attention from the crown of the head to the soles of the feet. Lovely lying down, before sleep or after a long day.

Read the guidance
  1. 0:00Welcome. Lie down or sit comfortably, and let your body be supported.
  2. 0:11Take three slow breaths, letting each exhale be a little longer.
  3. 0:26Bring your attention to the top of your head. Simply notice what is there.
  4. 0:40Let the forehead smooth. Soften the space between the eyebrows.
  5. 0:53Unclench the jaw. Let the tongue rest. Let the face be easy.
  6. 1:07Move down to the neck and shoulders. Let them drop, just a little.
  7. 1:23Feel your arms, heavy and resting. Notice the hands, and the fingertips.
  8. 1:40Bring awareness to the chest. Feel it rise and fall with each breath.
  9. 1:56Now the belly. Let it be soft. There's no need to hold it in.
  10. 2:12Notice your back, wherever it meets the chair, the floor, or the bed.
  11. 2:28Move to the hips. Let them grow heavy.
  12. 2:43Feel the thighs, the knees, and the lower legs. Let them be still.
  13. 3:00Bring your attention to your feet. The heels, the soles, the toes.
  14. 3:18Now sense the whole body at once. Breathing, resting, here.
  15. 3:36If any place feels tight, breathe toward it, and let it be as it is.
  16. 3:56You don't need to feel anything special. Noticing is enough.
  17. 4:14Take a deeper breath, and gently wiggle your fingers and toes.
  18. 4:30When you're ready, open your eyes, or let yourself drift toward sleep.
  19. 4:47Thank you for taking this time.

Sleep & rest · 5 minutes

Evening wind-down

Set the day down gently. A candlelit practice for releasing what's done, noticing what went well, and letting rest arrive.

Read the guidance
  1. 0:00Good evening. Let the day begin to settle, like dust in still air.
  2. 0:12Rest your eyes on the candle, or let them close.
  3. 0:23Breathe in gently, and breathe out slowly, as if fogging a window.
  4. 0:37Again, letting the out-breath be long and unhurried.
  5. 0:51Think of one thing from today that you can set down now.
  6. 1:06Imagine placing it on a shelf. It will be there in the morning, if you need it.
  7. 1:24Now think of one small thing that went well today.
  8. 1:39A kind word. A warm drink. A moment of quiet. Let it be enough.
  9. 1:57Let your shoulders lower. Let your hands grow warm and heavy.
  10. 2:15With each breath out, silently say: let go.
  11. 2:33Let go of the planning. Let go of the replaying.
  12. 2:51There is nothing left to do tonight. Rest is the only task.
  13. 3:10Feel the weight of your body, sinking, supported.
  14. 3:28Let your breath slow, all by itself.
  15. 3:48If thoughts arrive, let them drift past, like smoke from a candle.
  16. 4:08You have done enough today. You are allowed to rest.
  17. 4:28Stay here as long as you like, or let sleep come.
  18. 4:48Goodnight.

Loving-kindness · 4 minutes

Loving-kindness under open sky

Four minutes of warm wishes, offered to someone you love, to yourself, and outward to everyone beneath the same sky.

Read the guidance
  1. 0:00Welcome. Sit comfortably, and place a hand on your heart if you like.
  2. 0:10Take a slow breath, and let your face soften into a small smile.
  3. 0:22Bring to mind someone who makes you feel safe and cared for.
  4. 0:35Silently offer them: may you be happy.
  5. 0:45May you be healthy. May you be at ease.
  6. 0:57Now turn these wishes toward yourself, just as you are.
  7. 1:09May I be happy.
  8. 1:17May I be healthy.
  9. 1:25May I be at ease.
  10. 1:35If it feels awkward, that's alright. Simply say the words kindly.
  11. 1:50Bring to mind someone you barely know. A neighbour, or a shopkeeper.
  12. 2:04May you be happy. May you be healthy. May you be at ease.
  13. 2:20Now let your kindness spread outward, like light through clouds.
  14. 2:34To everyone in this town, this country, under this whole wide sky.
  15. 2:49May all beings be happy. May all beings be at ease.
  16. 3:06Rest in the warmth of these wishes for a few breaths.
  17. 3:25Notice how you feel now. Nothing to change.
  18. 3:39Gently return, carrying this kindness into your day.
  19. 3:52Thank you.
